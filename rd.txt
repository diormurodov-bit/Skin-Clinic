Skin—Clinic HTML Edition — Release Notes & Assets Manifest
=========================================================
Version: 1.4.0
API Base: https://api.wepro.uz/sandbox-shop

Included Pages:
- index.html      Home (Hero, Shop now -> products.html, Best sellers, Shop by category from backend, Featured products)
- products.html   Catalog with sticky desktop filter (bold active category selection, 0-100 price range)
- product.html    Product detail, qty selector, "In your bag" link notice, All Reviews & Leave a comment modal
- bag.html        Shopping cart (backend product thumbnails, links to checkout.html)
- checkout.html   Uniform English checkout form (desktop & mobile, pre-filled phone, Courier/Pickup + Map, gated Place Order submit, full-screen presentation video player with speed 0.5x-2x & sound controls)
- info.html       Support, Company, Legal & Start a Return (damaged product photo upload & description)
- login.html      Sign in (POST /login)
- register.html   Account registration (POST /register)
- profile.html    User profile & logout (GET /me, POST /logout)
